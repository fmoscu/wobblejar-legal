Wobblejar: Fruit Merge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Wobblejar: Fruit Merge (“Wobblejar”, “the app”) is a casual fruit merge puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Florian Moscu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. You do not need an account to play.

1. Your progress and saved games

Your progress, scores and settings are stored on your device (in the app’s local storage). We do not receive them and we have no user accounts or servers for this data.

So that you can get your progress back after reinstalling or on a new phone, the app also keeps a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, purchases made with coins and your album, but not your settings) in your own platform account: iCloud on iOS, and Google Play Games (Saved Games) on Android if you are signed in to Play Games. That copy is stored by Apple or Google under their privacy policies and we cannot access it. You can manage or delete it in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus (extra drops, doubled coins, free coins, a free booster or a second chance), and an occasional short full-screen ad between levels (rarely, and never in the middle of play). There are no banner ads. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before showing personalised ads we ask for your consent through Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → AD PRIVACY.
  • iOS: if the app asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), you can say Ask App Not to Track and still play; ads then are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Sharing your result

When you tap Share after the Daily Challenge, the app opens your device’s own share sheet with a short text (date, stars, fruit reached, drops and streak). Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it, and then it goes through the app you pick, under that app’s privacy policy.

4. Analytics and crash data

To understand how many people play, in which countries, and how far they get, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase: levels started and finished, stars, ads watched and similar game statistics, together with basic device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, country derived from your IP address) and a random app-instance identifier created by the app.

These statistics are not linked to your name, email or any account, are not used for advertising, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. The app does not give Google Analytics your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland they are only collected if you agree in Google’s consent message, and you can change your choice under Settings → AD PRIVACY. Google processes this data on our behalf and it is kept for no longer than 14 months. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy. The app does not send crash reports to us.

5. Children

Wobblejar is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through ads, you can use Google’s tools (myadcenter.google.com). If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you also have the right to object, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
