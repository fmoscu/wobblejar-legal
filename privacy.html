Wobblejar: Fruit Merge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Wobblejar: Fruit Merge (“Wobblejar”, “the app”) is a casual fruit merge puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Florian Moscu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app uses. You do not need an account to play.

1. We do not collect your data

The app does not collect, store on our side, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send anything over the internet.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, settings and collection are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). We never receive them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

3. Sharing your result

When you tap Share after the Daily Challenge, the app opens your device’s own share sheet with a short text (date, stars, fruit reached, drops and streak). Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it, and then it goes through the app you pick, under that app’s privacy policy.

4. Children

Wobblejar is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

5. Changes

If a future version adds anything that uses data (for example optional ads), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
